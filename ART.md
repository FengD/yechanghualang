# 卡面画作

[public/art/](public/art/) 含 8 幅为本项目生成的画作，每幅包含 160 像素缩略图和按需加载的 480 像素版本。用内置 ImageGen 根据下方文字提示生成两套四联作品，再裁出单幅并编码为 WebP。生成时只输入了文字提示。版次、系列与溢价由界面另外显示。

## 生成提示

**第一套**

> Generate ONE original square gallery print anthology contact sheet for a Chinese art-market browser game, built as an exact edge-to-edge 2 by 2 grid of four square framed-by-the-grid-only paintings. Exact straight central seams at 50% x and 50% y. Zero gutters, margins, frames, letters or text. Each quarter is a complete autonomous artwork with distinct colors, no element ever crosses a seam. Top left: warm orange vermilion pigment print, layered rocky mountains and a black sun, with artful woodcut forms. Top right: electric ultramarine marine abstraction, blue dark arches and a small cream geometric island, lithographic grain. Bottom left: olive green botanical silhouettes with chartreuse and pale chartreuse ink, retro screenprint leaf composition. Bottom right: plum purple and blush ink abstract city grid with offset playful geometry, dark currant highlights. All are newly invented museum-grade modernist images, no imitation of a specific artist or existing work. Compose each as a coherent real square art print. Subtle paper texture, large readable color blocks. No labels.

**第二套**

> One contact-sheet gallery artwork for an original online art auction game. Square image, perfectly equal 2 by 2 grid, hard seams exactly at halfway both ways, no border, no gutters, no frames, no text. Each cell is completely independent and a gallery-worthy modern pigment print. Cell upper left: peach-rust, charcoal, gold composition of a moon above terraced volcanic ridges, bold blocks. Cell upper right: navy, bright powder blue and coral scene with a harbor crane silhouetted against semicircular waves, clean reductive forms. Cell lower left: acid yellow and forest green inky seed pods on a florid modern screenprint, irregular contrasting marks. Cell lower right: mauve, lilac, butter white and burgundy painted checkerboard of cafe windows and reflections, elegant geometric abstract collage. Original designs, no emulation of an artist, no letters or labels. Let print texture and visible ink bring the images to life. No crossing the center seams.
